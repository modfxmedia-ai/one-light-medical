## **Why Does Knee Pain Get Worse With Age?**

As we get older, our bodies don’t bounce back like they used to. Joints like the knee take a lot of stress over the years, walking, running, climbing stairs, bending down, even just standing puts pressure on the joint. Over time, the cartilage that cushions your knee can wear away, leading to stiffness, swelling, and pain.

Here are some common reasons why **knee pain** increases as we age:

- **Cartilage Breakdown:** The smooth, rubbery tissue that helps your bones glide over each other starts to thin out, causing more friction and discomfort.
- **Decreased Blood Flow:** With age, your body’s circulation slows down, which affects how quickly your joints heal from stress or injury.
- **Inflammation:** Chronic inflammation becomes more common with age and can lead to joint damage if left untreated.
- **Osteoarthritis:** A degenerative joint condition that affects millions of older adults and is a major source of knee pain.

These age-related changes can lead to everything from occasional aches to daily pain that limits your ability to walk, exercise, or even sleep comfortably.
