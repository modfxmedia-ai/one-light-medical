## Do Not Let Summer Fun Become Summer Back Pain

Summer activities are great for your mood, but they can be rough on your back. In Amarillo, that might mean:

- Long days mowing, trimming, and hauling bags in the yard
- Bumpy drives to the lake or out-of-town vacations
- Extra sports like golf, pickleball, or softball
- Sitting in lawn chairs or on boat seats that offer almost no support

Add in the heat and dry air, and your body may feel sore faster. When you get warm, your muscles may feel looser at first, so it is easy to push harder without noticing strain. If you are even a little dehydrated, muscles and spinal joints may tighten and ache later in the day or at night.

If you spend most of the year sitting, your core and hips may be weaker than you think. Then, when you suddenly shovel, twist, reach, or lift more in summer, your back often pays the price. Working with a chiropractor in Amarillo before things get bad can help calm pain that is already there and lower your risk of getting sidelined all season.

## Why Summer Back Pain Hits So Hard in Amarillo

Many people feel like their back pain shows up right when they want to relax. That is not an accident. Common summer triggers include:

- Weekend warrior projects, like clearing out a garage in one day
- Long car rides with slouched posture or wallets in back pockets
- Sleeping on hotel or rental beds that feel nothing like your own
- Walking more on hard surfaces in flip-flops or flat sandals

Amarillo’s hot, dry climate can make these triggers worse. When you do not drink enough water, your body has a harder time cooling down and your muscles can fatigue faster. Stiff or tired muscles put more strain on the joints of your spine and can irritate nearby nerves.

It helps to know the difference between normal soreness and warning sign pain. Soreness usually feels dull, improves with light movement, and fades in a day or two. Warning sign pain might feel:

- Sharp or stabbing with certain movements
- Radiating into the leg, hip, or buttock
- Persistent, not improving, or waking you up at night

When pain acts like this, it is usually smarter to get evaluated instead of trying to push through. That way you are not guessing or waiting for something to “work itself out” while it quietly gets worse.

## Simple Summer Habits to Protect Your Back

There are simple habits that can make a big difference for your spine through the Amarillo summer. Some that we often recommend include:

- Drink water regularly, not just when you feel thirsty
- Take short breaks during yard work to reset posture and stretch
- Warm up briefly before golf, pickleball, or softball instead of going from zero to full speed

When lifting patio furniture, yard tools, or luggage, it helps to:

- Bend at your hips and knees, not your waist
- Keep the load close to your body
- Avoid twisting while you are holding something heavy, turn with your feet instead

If you work at a desk but are more active after hours in summer, try adding quick daily routines like:

- Core activation exercises to support your lower back
- Hip mobility drills to relieve strain on the spine
- Micro-breaks during the workday to stand, walk, and gently stretch

These simple steps, combined with care from a chiropractor in Amarillo, can help keep your back calmer through the hottest months.
