## What Exactly Is Red Light Therapy?

Red light therapy, sometimes called photobiomodulation or low-level laser therapy, uses specific wavelengths of red and near-infrared light to stimulate your body’s natural healing processes. The light penetrates deep into your skin and muscles, targeting the mitochondria—the energy-producing components of your cells.

When your cells absorb this light, they produce more ATP (adenosine triphosphate), which is essentially cellular fuel. This increase in energy accelerates tissue repair, reduces inflammation, and improves blood circulation.

Unlike medications, which may have side effects, or surgeries, which require recovery time, red light therapy is painless, non-invasive, and requires no downtime. You can literally stop in on your lunch break and head right back to your day feeling better.

## How Red Light Therapy Supports Muscle and Joint Recovery

### 1. Reducing Inflammation

After a workout, injury, or strain, your body’s natural reaction is to produce inflammation. While some inflammation is part of the healing process, too much can cause stiffness, pain, and delayed recovery. Red light therapy helps regulate inflammation by reducing pro-inflammatory cytokines and encouraging healthy cellular responses.

### 2. Enhancing Circulation

Improved circulation is crucial for recovery. Red light therapy stimulates the release of nitric oxide, a molecule that helps dilate blood vessels. This allows more oxygen and nutrient-rich blood to reach injured muscles and joints, speeding up repair and reducing soreness.

### 3. Accelerating Tissue Repair

Muscle fibers, ligaments, and tendons need energy to repair themselves. Red light therapy increases ATP production, giving your body the boost it needs to rebuild damaged tissue faster and more effectively.

### 4. Relieving Pain Naturally

By reducing inflammation and promoting circulation, red light therapy also helps ease pain without relying on painkillers. Many of our patients notice relief after just a few sessions, and the effects can be long-lasting.
