## What Red Light Therapy Is and How It Helps Your Body

Red light therapy, also called low-level light therapy or photobiomodulation, uses very specific wavelengths of red and near-infrared light. This light does not burn or cut the skin. Instead, it passes through the outer layers and is absorbed by your cells.

Inside each cell, tiny structures called mitochondria act like small power plants. When they absorb this kind of light, they may be able to make more ATP, which is the main form of cellular energy. With better energy inside the cells, the tissue can often do its normal repair work more effectively.

Possible benefits at the cellular level include:

- Support for healthier circulation in the treated area
- Support for reduced local inflammation
- Support for natural tissue repair in muscles and joints
- Support for more stable day-to-day energy

Red light therapy is generally:

- Non-invasive and non-surgical
- Done without needles or incisions
- Comfortable for most people, with no required downtime

During a session, you usually sit or lie in a relaxed position while the therapy devices are placed near the target area. You might feel gentle warmth, but not burning heat. As cellular function improves over time, many people notice less stiffness, easier movement, and support for overall metabolic health when they pair sessions with better food choices, hydration, and gentle activity.
