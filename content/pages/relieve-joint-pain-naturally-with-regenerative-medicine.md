## Understanding Joint Pain: Causes and Challenges

Joint pain can stem from a variety of causes, including:

**Osteoarthritis**: The gradual wear and tear of cartilage in the joints, often due to aging.

**Injuries**: Sprains, fractures, or trauma that lead to long-term joint damage.

**Inflammatory Conditions**: Diseases like rheumatoid arthritis or lupus that cause chronic inflammation.

**Overuse or Strain**: Repetitive motion or high-impact activities that stress the joints.

Regardless of the cause, the underlying issue often involves damaged or deteriorated tissue, such as cartilage, tendons, or ligaments. Traditional treatments like medications, physical therapy, or surgery may address symptoms but often fail to promote true healing. That’s where regenerative medicine steps in.
