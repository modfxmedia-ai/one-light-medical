## Why Headaches Often Start in the Spine

Most people think of headaches as something that happens inside the brain. But in reality, many headaches—especially chronic tension headaches and migraines—are caused by **nerve, muscle, and circulation issues in the spine**, particularly the neck (cervical region).

When your spine is misaligned:

- **Nerves are compressed** or irritated, sending pain signals to the head.
- **Muscles tighten**, pulling at the base of the skull and creating tension headaches.
- **Blood flow is restricted**, decreasing oxygen delivery to the brain and triggering migraines.

This means that the problem is often not “in your head,” but in the **structures supporting it**. That’s why people who sit all day, recover from car accidents, or have poor posture often notice their headaches are worse after long hours at a desk or physical strain.

## Warning Signs Your Headaches Are Spine-Related

Not every headache is linked to spinal misalignment, but there are clear red flags:

- Pain starts at the **base of the skull** and radiates upward.
- Headaches worsen after sitting at a desk or standing for long hours.
- You experience **neck stiffness or limited mobility** alongside the headache.
- Migraines improve slightly when posture is corrected.
- Headaches appear with **tingling, numbness, or weakness** in shoulders or arms.

If these symptoms sound familiar, you may be experiencing what we call **cervicogenic headaches**, which are caused by dysfunction in the neck and upper spine.

## The Cost of Ignoring Spine-Related Headaches

Many people live with headaches for years, treating them as a “normal” part of life. But ignoring spinal headaches can lead to bigger problems. Over time:

- **Nerve irritation becomes chronic**, leading to daily pain.
- **Postural changes worsen**, further stressing your neck and back.
- **Medication dependence increases**, with over-the-counter or prescription drugs leading to rebound headaches or harmful side effects.
- **Spinal degeneration progresses**, eventually causing long-term disability or requiring invasive surgery.

Every headache is a signal from your body that something deeper is wrong. Ignoring it allows the problem to escalate.
