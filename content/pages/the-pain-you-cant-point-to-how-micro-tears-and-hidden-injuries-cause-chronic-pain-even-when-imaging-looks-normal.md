## **The Science Behind Micro-Tears (and Why They Cause So Much Pain)**

Micro-tears are exactly what they sound like, microscopic damage to tissues.

But the impact is far from microscopic.

### **Here’s why they hurt so much:**

### **1. They create low-level inflammation that never shuts off.**

Your body keeps sending immune cells to “heal” the tissue, but because the damage is too small and too deep, it never fully resolves.

This creates **chronic inflammation**, which makes everything from movement to sleep more painful.

### **2. They disrupt normal muscle movement.**

When a muscle fiber is damaged, nearby fibers tighten to protect the area.

This leads to trigger points, knots, and tension that pulls your body out of alignment.

### **3. They irritate small sensory nerves.**

Even tiny tears expose nerve endings to inflammation.

This can create sharp pain, burning, tingling, or aching that radiates outward.

### **4. They weaken the tissue over time.**

If left untreated, micro-tears create weak points in muscles and tendons, making them more vulnerable to bigger injuries.

### **5. They create “mystery pain.”**

Because the tear is tiny and the surrounding tissues react, the pain often feels:

- Deep
- Hard to pinpoint
- Stiff
- Achy
- Worse with rest
- Worse with morning movement

Patients often say, “It hurts, but I can’t find exactly where.”

That’s classic micro-tear pain.
