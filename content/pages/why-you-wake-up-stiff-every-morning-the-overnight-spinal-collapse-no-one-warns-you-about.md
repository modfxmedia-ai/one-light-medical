## **The Real Reason You Wake Up Stiff: Your Spine “Shrinks” Overnight**

This surprises most patients.

While you sleep, your spinal discs, which act like shock absorbers, **lose fluid**.

Throughout the day, gravity pushes down on your spine, compressing your discs and joints. At night, your discs partially rehydrate… but **not fully**, especially if they are worn down, inflamed, or misaligned.

This lack of full rehydration leads to:

- Tight joints
- Reduced disc height
- Muscle stiffness
- Nerve sensitivity
- Poor spinal mobility

So the stiffness you feel in the morning isn’t random.

It’s a direct reflection of how your spine is functioning, and how much cumulative strain it’s under.

## **Common Causes of Morning Stiffness That Most People Never Realize**

There are many hidden reasons your spine feels stiff, achy, or locked-up when you wake up. Here are the most common, based on what we see every week in our clinic.

### **1. Joint Restriction in the Spine**

When the small joints of your spine (facet joints) can’t move freely, they become “stuck” overnight.

This causes stiffness every morning until you move enough to break through the restriction.

### **2. Spinal Misalignment**

Even small misalignments can create:

- Uneven pressure
- Muscle imbalance
- Nerve irritation
- Poor circulation

Misalignment means your spine never fully relaxes, not even during sleep.

### **3. Inflammation That Pools Overnight**

Inflammation from stress, injuries, or poor posture accumulates in joints and tissues.

While you’re still for 6–8 hours, that inflammation becomes stagnant, making the morning extremely painful.

### **4. Degenerative Disc Disease**

This doesn’t mean your spine is “falling apart.”

It means the discs are thinning and dehydrating, causing:

- Less cushioning
- More friction
- Increased stiffness
- Reduced flexibility

Morning pain becomes one of the earliest signs.

### **5. Tight Muscles From Poor Posture**

People underestimate how much posture affects their sleep and morning mobility.

When your posture is off, the muscles around your spine tighten to compensate, and they stay tight all night.

### **6. Nerve Irritation From Sleep Position**

If your neck or lower back nerves are irritated, sleeping can magnify the problem.

You may wake up with:

- Numbness
- Burning
- Tingling
- Shooting pain
- Stiffness
- Weakness

These aren’t “random.” They’re nerve signals.

### **7. Lack of Movement Overnight**

Your spine thrives on movement.

But while you sleep, your joints stay still for hours, causing:

- “Locked” joints
- Tight ligaments
- Stiff muscles
- Slow circulation

This is why movement in the morning sometimes helps, but only temporarily.
